PRIVACY POLICY

Dozer game

Effective date: 21 November 2026

1. General

This Policy explains what data is processed when you play the game Dozer (the "Game") and how it is protected. The Game is developed by vs_developer (the "Developer"). Contact: vs_developer@inbox.ru.

The Developer does not run its own servers, accounts or personal data databases. Everything the Game saves itself stays on your device.

2. Data stored only on the device

The Game stores locally: level progress, stars and best results, the number of lives and hints, music and sound settings, daily task and achievement counters, and the selected interface language.
This data is never sent to the Developer and is removed together with the app or when you clear its data in Android settings.

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads through Yandex Mobile Ads (Yandex LLC). To select and display ads, the advertising SDK may process: the device advertising identifier (GAID/OAID), IP address, device information, OS and app version, language and region, and ad impression and interaction events.
This processing is governed by the Yandex privacy policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/
You can opt out of ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. App store services

The Google Play build uses Google Play Games Services (leaderboard and achievements). If you sign in to Play Games, Google processes your game profile, achievements and leaderboard scores in accordance with the Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Sign-in only happens when you choose it; without signing in, achievements stay local.
The RuStore build uses RuStore services to check for updates and request a review. The related data is processed by RuStore under the documents published at rustore.ru.
The Game has no in-app purchases.

5. Permissions

The Game requests internet access and network state access, which are needed for ads and app store services. Service permissions of the advertising SDK and store services are also used: the advertising ID (AD_ID) and app install attribution (install referrer); in the RuStore build, background update checks, including after a device reboot. Contacts, camera, microphone, location and files are not requested.

6. Sharing with third parties

The Developer does not sell or share user data. Data is processed only by the services listed above (Yandex, Google, RuStore) to the extent required for their operation.

7. Children

The Game is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child provided such data, email vs_developer@inbox.ru and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Local data is protected by Android app sandboxing. Data transferred by advertising and game services is sent over secure connections (HTTPS) by those services.

9. Your rights

You can delete local data by clearing the app data or uninstalling the Game; disable ad personalization in Android settings; sign out of Play Games and delete game data in your Google account settings. Send data-related requests to vs_developer@inbox.ru.

10. Changes to this policy

This Policy may be updated. The current version is always available at the URL listed in the Game's store listing. The effective date is shown at the top of this document.

11. Contact

E-mail: vs_developer@inbox.ru
